# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **Fabiq Smart Factory MES Integration Platform** project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to providing a welcoming environment regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- Keeping discussions professional and technical

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Insulting or derogatory comments
- Personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Disruptive behavior in issues, pull requests, or discussions

---

## Project Scope

This project is focused on smart manufacturing, MES, Industrial IoT, event-driven architecture, and software engineering practices.

Discussions should remain constructive and relevant to:

- Code quality
- Documentation
- Architecture
- Deployment
- Manufacturing concepts
- Developer experience
- Security and reliability

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying expected standards of behavior
- Taking appropriate corrective action when necessary
- Removing, editing, or rejecting contributions that violate this code
- Moderating issues, discussions, and pull requests when needed

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer.

Reports should include:

- Description of the incident
- Relevant links, screenshots, or messages
- Names or usernames involved
- Any additional context that may help with review

Maintainers will review and respond in a fair and timely manner.

---

## Enforcement Actions

Depending on the severity and frequency of the behavior, maintainers may take actions such as:

1. Private warning
2. Public clarification
3. Temporary restriction from participation
4. Permanent removal from the project community
